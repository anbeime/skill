小红书社媒卡。整体采用“一大一组”的非对称结构。顶部设置 1–2 行醒目的短标题，形成独立标题区域，并与下方主体保持明显间距。中部设置一个占据主要面积的大型主视觉，整体偏向画面一侧；在相对另一侧集中安排 4–6 个尺寸明显更小的信息单元，形成连续、紧凑的次级信息组。主视觉可以位于左侧或右侧，信息组随之镜像调整，不固定具体方向。小信息单元不环绕主视觉四周，也不分散到画面两侧，可上下错落、两列穿插或疏密变化；每个单元由一个小视觉元素与极短文字组成。主视觉区域约占中部内容面积的 55%–70%，信息组约占 25%–40%，两者通过留白自然分隔，不使用分栏线、统一卡片框、表格或规则宫格。底部可保留一行很短的总结文字。整体强调“一个大视觉块＋一个集中小信息簇”，版面非对称但稳定，避免中心辐射、四周环绕、平均分布或满画面散点排版。

Xiaohongshu social-media card. Use an asymmetrical “one large visual plus one group” structure. Place a prominent one- or two-line short headline in a dedicated top title area, clearly separated from the body below. In the middle, place one large primary visual occupying most of the content area and weighted toward one side; concentrate 4–6 much smaller information units in one continuous, compact secondary group on the opposite side. The primary visual may be on the left or right, with the information group mirrored accordingly rather than fixed to one direction. Do not scatter the small units around the hero or across both sides. Arrange them with staggered rows, interleaved columns, or varied density; each unit consists of a small visual element and extremely short text. Let the primary visual occupy about 55%–70% of the middle content area and the information group about 25%–40%, separating them through whitespace rather than divider lines, uniform card frames, tables, or regular grids. A very short summary line may appear at the bottom. Emphasize “one large visual block plus one concentrated small information cluster”: asymmetrical but stable, avoiding radial layouts, all-around placement, even distribution, or scattered elements across the whole canvas.
