小红书社媒卡。整体采用极简的大留白结构，以一组大字号短文案作为绝对视觉主体。主文案放置在画面上半部或中上部，占据主要横向宽度，可使用 2–3 行自然换行；文字整体偏左排列，但不紧贴边缘，形成稳定的大块文字区域。主文案字号明显大于其他元素，行距相对紧凑，个别关键词可通过颜色、字重、底纹或轻微标记进行局部强调，但不要让多个词同时抢夺注意力。主文案下方保留较大的空白距离，在中央或略偏一侧放置一个尺寸很小的视觉点缀，作为第二视觉停顿，不承担主要信息。画面四角可以加入极少量微型辅助信息，例如日期、页码、符号或装饰点，字号和视觉重量都非常低。整体不设置卡片框、分栏、信息列表或复杂图文组合，背景保持简洁，强调大文字、少元素、大留白、弱装饰，形成轻盈安静、适合快速阅读的社媒封面感。

Xiaohongshu social-media card. Use an extremely minimal, spacious layout with a short piece of large typography as the absolute visual focus. Place the main copy in the upper or upper-middle area, spanning most of the available width and naturally wrapping across two or three lines. Align the text generally to the left without pressing it against the edge, creating a stable block. Make the main copy much larger than every other element with relatively tight line spacing. Emphasize only selected keywords through color, weight, a subtle background mark, or a light accent; do not let multiple words compete for attention. Leave generous space below the copy and place one very small visual accent in the center or slightly to one side as a secondary visual pause, without carrying primary information. The four corners may contain only a few tiny supporting details such as a date, page number, symbol, or decorative dot, all with very low visual weight. Do not use card frames, columns, information lists, or complex text-image combinations. Keep the background simple and emphasize large type, few elements, generous whitespace, and restrained decoration for a light, quiet, fast-to-read social cover.
