Xiaohongshu social-media card. Use a clear three-level structure of “top headline + large central person + scattered labels on both sides.” Place one prominent short headline at the top in the largest type, centered or slightly left-aligned. Put one complete person in the center as the absolute visual focus; the person should occupy about 60%–75% of the image height, extend from the upper-middle area toward the bottom, and retain a complete silhouette. Distribute 3–5 extremely short labels on both sides, each limited to one line or a short phrase, with brief thin lines, slashes, or leader lines pointing toward nearby parts of the person. Stagger and distribute the labels freely rather than as a regular list, while keeping left-right balance. Leave generous space between the person and text without covering the subject. Preserve a largely empty background and avoid complex graphic blocks, card frames, tables, or extra decoration for immediate mobile readability.
