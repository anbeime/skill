Xiaohongshu social-media card with generous negative space. Place a prominent one- or two-line short headline at the top, clearly separated from the main subject area. In the middle and lower area, place one large primary visual occupying most of the composition; it may be centered, left-weighted, or right-weighted rather than locked to one position. Let the primary visual's silhouette, pose, and spatial footprint naturally create several areas of open space. Insert 4–6 smaller information units into those open spaces, each made of a small visual element and extremely short text. Let their positions follow the space left by the primary visual rather than preset columns; do not distribute them evenly around the subject or create regular side-by-side columns. Keep the small units clearly smaller than the primary visual and close to the gaps created by its outline, with varied vertical positions and density. Do not use uniform card frames, regular grids, tables, or obvious divider lines. A very short summary sentence may appear at the bottom as a closing element. Prioritize the main visual, let whitespace drive the placement, and weave information into the composition so it reads as one complete scene rather than assembled modules.
