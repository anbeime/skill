小红书社媒卡。排版采用中心主视觉环绕式结构：顶部设置两级短标题，其中主标题醒目。画面中央放置一个占比最大的主体人物或物体，作为绝对视觉中心；主体四周分散安排 3–6 个尺寸较小的独立插图，每个小图旁仅配一个极短标签或事项名称。小图采用自由、不规则的环绕分布，左右和下方错落排布，保持视觉平衡而非整齐网格。背景保留大面积留白，主图、小图、文字之间保持明显间距，形成“一大主体＋多个小信息点”的层级关系。

Xiaohongshu social-media card. Use a central-hero surrounding layout: place two levels of short headlines at the top, with the main headline prominent. Put one large person or object in the center as the absolute visual focus. Distribute 3–6 smaller independent illustrations around it, each accompanied by only an extremely short label or item name. Arrange the small illustrations freely and irregularly, staggered on the left, right, and below the hero rather than in a neat grid, while maintaining visual balance. Preserve generous negative space and clear gaps between the hero, small illustrations, and text to create a hierarchy of “one large subject plus multiple small information points.”
