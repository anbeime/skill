## 💡 Tip 1: The Universal Poster Mindset

What if the preset infographic types in the hand-drawn skill aren't enough? Treat the infographic as a poster. Simply tell the skill the information you want to present and the scenario, and let the skill handle the rest:

```text
做一张《山系露营轻量化装包》海报，把装备按睡眠系统（帐篷/睡袋）、烹饪系统（炉头/钛杯）、照明系统（马灯）分类，标注总重量控制在 8kg 内，户外手绘工装风。
```

<a id="tip-2-style-gacha"></a>
## 🤖 Tip 2: Style Gacha

Too many styles and don't feel like choosing? Try pulling a gacha card:

```text
帮我生成猫咪在窗台晒太阳的治愈插画提示词，你帮我选风格和主题色（可组合）
```

<a id="tip-3-the-precise-assembly-method"></a>
## 📐 Tip 3: The Precise Assembly Method

Combine layout, style, and theme color freely. If style or color is omitted, append "pick the rest for me":

```text
Layout: SC-001, Style: 041, Theme color: C-01, Theme: First milk tea of autumn.
```
